Madhuchista Vidhana • GI-Tagged Guilds

The Sacred Alchemy of Traditional Craftsmanship

Every DivyaKala creation passes through three sacred stages of handwork—preserving techniques documented in the Chola-era Manasara Shilpa Shastra.

01
Swamimalai, Tamil Nadu

Lost-Wax Madhuchista Casting

Each idol is first sculpted by hand in pure beeswax and dammar resin, encased in alluvial Cauvery river clay, and poured with molten virgin brass—meaning every single mold is broken to birth one unique murti.

02
Kumbakonam & Varanasi

Fine Nakshi Chisel & Repoussé

Using over 40 micro-chisels, master engravers carve lotus petals, Ashtamangala emblems, and sacred mantras into solid brass and electrolytic copper before buffing with natural tamarind and wood ash.

03
Mysuru & Vrindavan

Sacred Floral & Resin Alchemy

Blessed marigold, rose, and parijata garlands from sanctums are sun-dried, stone-milled, and hand-rolled with forest Halmaddi resin, Bhimseni camphor, and Mysore sandalwood oil.

Our Heritage Story • Founded 1984

Four Decades of Safeguarding India’s Sacred Metal & Ritual Arts

Founded along the sacred ghats of Varanasi and the bronze foundries of Kumbakonam in 1984, DivyaKala began with a single vow: to ensure that families establishing home mandirs never have to settle for hollow, machine-pressed imitations.

Today, every idol, lamp, and pooja thali we ship carries the hallmark of its creator guild, accompanied by energized Kashi Vibhuti and our lifetime brass authenticity guarantee.
