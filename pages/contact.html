Frequently Asked Questions

All DivyaKala Swamimalai and Kumbakonam idols are 100% solid lost-wax cast (Ghana-madhuchista) using virgin brass or traditional Panchaloha alloy. According to Agama Shastra, hollow idols should not be worshipped in a home sanctum; hence we never sell hollow or scrap-filled castings.
Every Rudraksha mala and collector bead undergoes digital X-ray verification to confirm natural internal seed compartments (mukhis) without artificial carving or glue tampering. A physical lab certificate accompanies your shipment.
Avoid harsh chemical polishes. Gently rub pure brass and copper vessels with natural tamarind paste (Puli) or lemon juice mixed with fine sea salt and Pitambari powder, rinse in warm water, and wipe dry immediately with a soft cotton cloth.
Ready-to-ship items dispatch within 24 hours and arrive across India in 3–5 business days (5–8 days globally via DHL). In the rare event of transit damage, we provide a 100% free replacement within 7 days. Customer contact details are strictly protected and never shared with third parties.
