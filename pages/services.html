Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

How far in advance should we schedule a Purohit for Griha Pravesh? ▼

We recommend booking at least 7 to 14 days in advance to allow our Acharyas to verify auspicious muhurtas and assemble fresh, personalized samagri. However, urgent requests for sudden muhurtas can be accommodated within 48 hours based on scholar availability.

Can families residing outside India participate via live digital stream? ▼

Yes. We host live, interactive private video ceremonies from our consecrated temple atelier in Kumbakonam and Varanasi. Priests recite your family’s exact Gotra, Nakshatra, and Sankalpa, with two-way audio allowing you to chant along. The consecrated prasadam is vacuum-sealed and dispatched via express international courier.

What metallurgy standards are used in custom deity casting? ▼

Our Sthapati guilds work strictly in accordance with Agama and Shilpa Shastras using virgin Panchaloha (copper, brass, lead-free zinc, silver, and gold trace) or solid bronze. Every idol undergoes non-destructive XRF spectrometry testing, and comes with a certified metal assay certificate.

Is all ritual samagri provided, or do we need to buy items locally? ▼

Every Purohit booking includes our complete, sealed Sampoorna Samagri Kit containing all 32+ required sacred ingredients (organic herbs, dry fruits, unadulterated cow ghee, camphor, navadhanyam, and sacred threads). You only need to arrange fresh flowers and seasonal fruits.
